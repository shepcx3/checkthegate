About

On a film set, "check the gate" is the last look through the camera before the shot is called done. We work that way on everything.

Check the Gate is a production company based in Kingston, Ontario. We handle a project from the first idea to the final file: writing, shooting, editing, color, and sound.

Crews are sized to the job, from a two-person documentary team to a full commercial unit. You see cuts along the way, and nothing ships until we've checked it twice.
