How a project goes

  1. Plan

    We start with a call about what you need the film to do. Then we write the treatment, build the schedule, and lock a budget you can count on.

  2. Shoot

    We bring a crew sized to the job, from a two-person documentary team to a full commercial unit, and we check every setup before we move on.

  3. Finish

    Edit, color, and sound all happen in-house. You see cuts along the way and get final files ready for wherever the film is headed.
